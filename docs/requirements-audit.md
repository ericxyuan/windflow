# Windflow requirements audit

This register compares the complete original desktop-fan brief with the current artifacts and the later GitHub, screen and encoder instructions. It records implementation at its actual level: source, generated geometry, imported assembly, analytical circuit or measured hardware. It is not a manufacturing release.

The pushed screen/control checkpoint is `8ede72d`; the reviewed interaction improvements are pushed as `422bd7b`. They include a 24-detent normal-power range, clearer screen hierarchy, readiness prompts and cosmetic-settings handling. Current workspace work adds the routed encoder board, mechanical carrier/interface study, corrected fan stack, M1.6 Pico mounts and grille interlock. The control/UI/service changes pass source tests, production rendering and Pico compilation recorded in the build provenance. Independent board/study checks do not establish a current complete assembly pass; adoption and whole-product checking remain necessary. Use [deliverable status](deliverable-status.md) and [build provenance](../firmware/dist/build-validation.json) for current counts and hashes; this audit deliberately does not duplicate changing test totals.

## Status meanings

| Status | What it establishes |
|---|---|
| Implemented | The stated source, geometry or document exists and has the indicated verification. It does not establish physical performance. |
| Partial | A useful implementation exists, but an achievable design or integration requirement remains open. |
| Unfinished design | Required circuit, geometry, routing, mating or assembly work is absent or not adopted. This work can continue without physical hardware. |
| Physical testing | The design has a starting value or analytical rationale; measurements are needed to qualify it. |
| In progress | A current workspace change or independent study is not yet accepted into the checked complete assembly/release. |

## Later instructions that change the original brief

| Later instruction | Current treatment |
|---|---|
| GitHub username is `ericxyuan` | The canonical project URL is [ericxyuan/windflow](https://github.com/ericxyuan/windflow). Active repository/document links were updated. Historical evidence remains historical. |
| Replace multiple front light bars with a clear screen | Adafruit 4311, 320 × 240 landscape IPS, replaces the main speed pixels and separate RGB status lamp. The downward ambient strip remains. The screen has a clear protective window, not an LED diffuser. |
| Reserve the top screen section for the replaced light-bar functions | The top 64 rows show normal power, boost and power/system/fault information. |
| A full turn after normal maximum enters boost control | Normal maximum keeps the panels open. A fresh 24-detent / 360° upward entry turn enables boost at zero closure; subsequent rotation changes boost. Crossing an ordinary percentage alone cannot enter boost. |
| A new full turn is required after leaving boost | Returning boost to zero exits boost control and resets entry progress. Off, fault, service and reboot also clear authorization. Re-entry cannot accumulate while the panels are reopening or safety limits are active. |
| Show encoder position and distance to boost entry | The screen shows relative wheel phase and remaining degrees. The incremental encoder cannot measure an absolute shaft angle; its relative reference is explicitly documented. |

The full-turn instruction supersedes the original single continuous normal/boost threshold behavior. `boostThreshold` remains an internal stored-setting split, not permission to constrict the outlet. Likewise, a saved boost request restores normal maximum with open panels instead of bypassing the required new entry turn.

## Ordered completion work and acceptance

1. **Accept the control/UI/service revision.** Repeat control/input/display/service tests and the Pico build; regenerate the production-rendered preview; compare source/UF2 hashes. Demonstrate 24 normal detents from zero to maximum, 23 entry detents without closure, the 24th enabling boost at zero, progressive subsequent closure, fresh entry after exit, and safe blocked-entry prompts. Cosmetic changes must preserve valid mechanical commissioning; unsafe changes must still clear affected evidence.
2. **Adopt the fan stack and physical interfaces.** Include four limiters, real head/washer/thread envelopes, tubing, interlock/contact travel, clamps and service slack. No known nominal collision may be left as a physical uncertainty. Check insertion and tool approaches with the actual occupied paths.
3. **Finish the electrical design.** Adopt one real module interconnection scheme, capture the circuit and reference designators, complete ERC/DRC, route current/thermal paths, and generate fabrication/placement data for all custom boards. An electrically empty mechanical board is not an electrical acceptance result.
4. **Reconcile final CAD and purchasing data.** Integrate the populated boards/connectors/harness, finish Onshape mates/parameter behavior, repeat affected whole-assembly/service-path checks, and freeze the print manifest/BOM/fastener schedule against that revision.
5. **Print and dry-assemble the controlled prototype.** Resolve coupon fit, layers/supports, insert strength, grille operation, free mechanism travel and one-handed control stability before powering the loads. Record failed fits and change geometry rather than forcing assembly.
6. **Qualify electronics and performance on hardware.** Verify rails and transients first, then commission sensors/servo/tach, perform controlled faults, and measure flow/throw/noise/thermal behavior/endurance under [the physical plan](physical-validation.md). Report raw measurements and uncertainty, including comparisons with and without the straightener.

The project should be considered complete to the extent possible without hardware only after items 1–4 have accepted artifacts and all remaining item 5–6 dependencies are explicitly recorded as physical work. At the current review those achievable design items remain open.
