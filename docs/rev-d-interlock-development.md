# Rev D grille interlock development

The independent adaptation in `cad/rev_d_interlock.py` passed **1,233 nominal part comparisons with zero collisions** and exit code zero against the source-matched 65-group stage. It remains a separate pilot in `cad/rev_d/interlock-study`; it is **not** included in the current main assembly or Onshape version.

The selected switch is **Omron D2F-01L**, with a low-level contact. COM and NO follow the current electrical wiring; NC stays insulated. The case, lever and three terminals are drawing envelopes. They do not simulate the snap action, spring return, contact timing or elastic overtravel. The integral grille tongue has 4 mm nominal root embed. The intended adjustment is ±2 mm along airflow, with 0.25 mm nominal post-trip travel and 0.20 mm additional stop travel, below the drawing's 0.55 mm minimum overtravel. Those inputs require actual switch calibration before use.

The switch case center is (−60.2, 255.05, 18.4) mm. Its front actuator contact datum is Y = 259.85 mm, outside the useful outlet. The revised lower raceway anchor stays at least 0.3 mm outboard of the 104 mm panel width, while connecting to the rounded duct corner. A broader first anchor touched the open lower panel; that interference was corrected geometrically rather than excluded from checking. The protective cover ends behind the fixed grille seat and has an open lower inboard access window. All printed pilot parts must remain single connected solids.

The two insulated signal routes use a 1.1 mm envelope, separate 1.2 mm liner holes and a 14 mm bend input. The sampled minimum centerline radius is approximately 13.91 mm. The selected [Alpha Wire 5853 specification](https://www.alphawire.com/en/products/wire/hook-up-wire/premium/5853) gives 0.039 ± 0.004 inch outside diameter and a minimum bend radius of ten times outside diameter. At the maximum stated diameter, that is 10.922 mm. The route begins beyond each terminal tip; bare solder bonds, connector transitions, complete main-harness routing and service slack are not modeled by this pilot.

Reproduce with the pinned CAD environment using `cad/rev_d_interlock.py`, then `tools/verify_rev_d_interlock_mesh.py`. The nominal report records every source and STEP hash. Mesh checks apply only to the six isolated printed bracket, cover, clamp, raceway, lid and TPU liner outputs.

The next authorized work is to check grille withdrawal, bracket adjustment, boost motion, wheel press and screen service against these changes, then resolve the screw/nut stacks and driver approaches. In particular, a straight side approach to the present X-axis bracket screws would meet the outer enclosure; service access must be redesigned or verified with a concrete tool path. This is unfinished mechanical work, not a claim that a future physical test alone will solve access. Changed head/grille geometry needs fresh complete stage, mesh, motion and export reports before integration online.

Physical checks remain switch travel/return, contact debounce and hardwired inhibit, wire/liner grip, strain relief, repeated grille removal, magnet retention and residual rotor coast. The fixed printed finger guard is not qualified fragment containment for the experimental rotor.
