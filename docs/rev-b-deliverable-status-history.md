# Deliverable status — 5 October 2026

**Supersession notice, 6 October 2026:** the active motor, impeller, ESC, power and flowing-head redesign is Rev C. See [its implementation plan](rev-c-implementation-plan.md), [motor and power specification](rev-c-motor-power.md), [impeller](rev-c-impeller.md) and [firmware](rev-c-firmware.md). The register below records the earlier Rev B state. Its assembly clearance results and 12 V fan circuit are not evidence for Rev C. Current native Onshape and local Rev C geometry are development artifacts; purchased-component integration, retention, guards, mechanism details and whole-assembly validation must be completed before print release.

This is a progress register, not a manufacturing release or completion certificate. Onshape access and file upload are working. The original design is preserved in its baseline version. The latest user revision replaces the two main LED sticks and status lamp with a 320 x240 IPS screen, retains downward ambient lighting, and requires a full encoder revolution before each boost session. Current engineering work includes that concrete CAD, firmware and electrical integration.

## Verified evidence and its limits

- Current E3 firmware: 41,311 control assertions, 185 display-view assertions, 227 production service-command assertions and 384,000 exact production Adafruit_GFX pixel comparisons, each with exit 0. Final Pico compilation exits 0: 121,460 program bytes, 11,112 static RAM bytes, plus a 10,240-byte heap canvas. These are source/build/render results, not attached-electronics testing.
- Current display service path: 174 samples, zero unintended intersections, exit 0; generator hashes match the checked sources. The sequence removes the front bezel/window, pulls the unplugged cradle rearward 2 mm and lowers it through the bottom after tray removal. The check excludes screws, tools, harness and tolerances. Bezel uses four RX-M2x4 inserts rather than tapped PETG.
- Display fastener supplement: twelve nominal ISO 4762 screw-head/rear-tip envelopes clear the installed base components with exit 0. This does not check intentional thread/insert engagement, hand access, a complete driver sweep or tolerance extremes.
- Onshape: native airflow studio shows seven features and five parts. Nozzle boost fractions 0, 0.5 and 1 regenerate; a 0.5 minimum area ratio is rejected by the 15 degree angle ceiling. Saved setting: 0 boost, 0.75 minimum area. This study does not drive the imported structural shell automatically.
- Housing import: exact eight-arc circular throat loft replaces the near-circle sliver geometry. Left and right halves each show one part with **Allow faulty parts unchecked**. Proof images are under `cad/evidence/`.
- Local mechanism fixture: 61 nominal positions, zero unintended overlap. Thirteen sampled insertion paths pass with exit 0 after changing crank approach to vertical. The insertion model excludes fasteners, servo, wiring, guards and tools installed later.
- The revised local base generator passes its support/component checks, and the display service check passes against that regenerated base. The interlock is being adopted in the head. No current whole-assembly pass is claimed until all adopted geometry is rebuilt and source/input hashes match a new report.
- The **3 October E3 checkpoint** passed with exit 0: **76 components, 5,536 pair checks and 183 cover-removal samples** across open/halfway/maximum boost, with zero unintended intersections. Its imported STEP SHA256 is `9e377ef335463d43efa9764526675d6dbd54f98391e9d279571e6ecb914d7159`. That report is historical evidence for that checkpoint, not the subsequently changed workspace geometry. It excludes complete fastener, harness, tool and tolerance validation.
- The 3 October Onshape checkpoint has 76 instances and seven native interference entries: six are internal contacts within the vendor HUSB238 model, and one is intentional encoder/thumbwheel engagement. No other findings were reported for that revision. These imported nominal solids do not establish motion mates, print tolerances or physical assembly qualification.
- Encoder PCB P1: editable native schematic and routed two-layer board, exact four-net pin checks, zero ERC/DRC/unconnected/parity findings and clean command exits. Its independent [Onshape datum studio](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/5ca1b5b26cd9a4a93dde4737/w/6d3d38529391b53ad011b362/e/21041dd778175eaad866a4ab) visibly contains one part. Actual seating, soldering and thumbwheel force remain physical checks.
- Grille interlock study: 18,737 nominal checks, 61 panel poses, 5,002 panel comparisons and eight driver approaches pass with clean exit 0. The documented solder/TPU compression contacts are intentional. The study uses frozen inputs and requires main assembly adoption, base continuation and physical overtravel/contact tests.
- Nominal gross outlet area is 9,776 mm² open and 7,332 mm² at the initial limit. These dimensions establish a geometric contraction, not higher measured jet speed or throw.

## Engineering still required before physical qualification

1. Complete assembly motion mates, harness and tool-access review. Recheck and import the newly adopted fan stack, Pico mounting, encoder PCB and grille interlock. The 76-instance E3 import is an older checked checkpoint; populated service paths and tolerance extremes remain open.
2. Adopt the carrier-to-Pico/regulator physical interconnection. The native 90 × 65 × 1.6 mm mechanical PCB has four matching NPTH holes and enforced mounting keepouts; it passes 23 native checks, zero-violation baseline DRC and three deliberate negative keepout probes. Its independent Onshape studio contains one part. It has no electrical circuit. The separate keyed ribbon study passes 555 nominal checks against the revised base, but still needs bend, clamp and service-slack validation. See `hardware/pcb/carrier-mechanical/README.md` and `hardware/carrier-interface-study.md`.
3. Finish the captured main-carrier circuit, route its PCB, run ERC/DRC and generate fabrication/placement files. The encoder board is routed and checked; the direct-solder pressure daughterboard is being captured and routed.
4. Complete pressure-tap hose connection, tube path, grille-present switch mount/contact interface, wiring restraints and service slack. Resolve the fan compression-limiter/pad interface and model its actual stack.
5. Reconcile final fastener quantities and demonstrate tool access and populated-tray removal. Run final source-matched interference and insertion checks after those changes.

These are unfinished design tasks, not merely uncertainties delegated to physical testing. Airflow, noise, finger access, magnetic retention, print fit, servo friction, fault timing, thermal behavior and USB margins remain separate physical qualification work.
