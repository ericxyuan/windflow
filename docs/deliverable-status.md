Current nominal validation checks 5,778 part pairs with two declared thermal insert seats. Complete motion checks 59,558 comparisons, with zero unintended intersections; a further 196,561 current cartridge checks cover operation, adjustment, removal and tool paths. The exact 195 mm complete-height box passes. All 34 stage STLs and the current USB and insert-seat coupons pass topology checks. The [exchange report](../cad/rev_d/integration-export-validation.json) confirms 108 named groups / 208 local round-trip solids and a 0.00685 mm³ volume difference. These results apply only to their recorded source/output hashes and do not qualify physical fit or operation.

The [authorized unfinished queue](rev-d-requirements-audit.md) remains active. Unfinished electronics, installed cable strain relief, wires/hoses and fastener/tool work are design items, not merely future physical tests. No overall completion or manufacturing release is claimed.
