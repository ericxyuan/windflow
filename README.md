# Windflow desktop fan

Rev D is the current **150 mm CAD development candidate**, following the saved rotor-selection study. It uses one **T-Motor Pacer V4 P2406 Juicy 2060KV**, a custom swept/twisted five-blade impeller, one telemetry ESC, a flowing hollow enclosure and an integrated progressive outlet. Electronics retain the Rev C baseline. The active firmware, in the historical `WindflowRevC` folder, now binds calibration to the exact 150 mm rotor article with schema 5; rotor motion remains inhibited until physical commissioning requirements are met. The earlier 112 mm geometry is preserved.

Start with the [150 mm CAD decisions, validation, printing limits and remaining work](docs/rev-d-150mm-cad.md), [rotor parameters](cad/rev_d/rotor-parameters.json), [enclosure parameters](cad/rev_d/head-parameters.json) and [native editable Onshape rotor](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/5ca1b5b26cd9a4a93dde4737/w/6d3d38529391b53ad011b362/e/ac831cfb3e2f99f349f8306c). This is a development revision; no operating speed is approved for the larger rotor.

The [native adjustable airpath](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/5ca1b5b26cd9a4a93dde4737/w/6d3d38529391b53ad011b362/e/399cdd4584e017c0a30623b4) now contains seven major-profile parts and 17 inputs. [Installed front, inlet, internal and control/port review views](cad/rev_d/preview-record.json) show the separate 65-group fit geometry, including the [retained side-facing USB-C port and fit coupon](docs/rev-d-usb-access.md). Renders and native study checks do not qualify the physical fan.

The canonical repository is [ericxyuan/windflow](https://github.com/ericxyuan/windflow). The user's existing Onshape document now contains the [65-instance Rev D detailed development assembly](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/5ca1b5b26cd9a4a93dde4737/w/6d3d38529391b53ad011b362/e/57c900ab51f3a2c24ffaefca), alongside the separate native rotor and airpath studies. The [current online checkpoint](cad/onshape-rev-d-checkpoint.json) identifies the matching STEP, immutable version and screenshot evidence. Onshape's whole-assembly interference check returned six contacts entirely within the supplied HUSB238 model and no custom-part findings. The original dual-fan and earlier Rev B/C designs remain preserved.

The protected IPS screen replaces the front speed and status light bars. Its top 64 rows retain that status role; the main view shows normal power, relative wheel phase and the degrees remaining before boost control. A 24-detent turn covers normal output. At maximum normal output, another **fresh full turn** enters boost control while the panels remain open; subsequent rotation progressively closes them. Every exit, off/fault or reboot requires a fresh entry turn. The encoder sits beside the screen, turning parallel to its surface. Downward ambient lighting and long-press night mode remain. See [front controls](docs/rev-c-front-controls.md).
