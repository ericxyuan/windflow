# Windflow desktop fan

Engineering prototype in progress, 2026-10-03. **This is not a manufacturing release.**

The canonical repository is [ericxyuan/windflow](https://github.com/ericxyuan/windflow). The current [76-instance Onshape assembly — IPS screen and full-turn boost](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/5ca1b5b26cd9a4a93dde4737/w/6d3d38529391b53ad011b362/e/9ee256003ae02b710e21a9ec) includes the E3 display revision and purchased-component models. The original [Onshape design](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/5ca1b5b26cd9a4a93dde4737/w/6d3d38529391b53ad011b362/e/4ed72084c2e4978faf960789) is preserved in **Baseline - original dual fan and iris - 2026-09-08**. The **Rev B — Native parametric airflow** studio contains a rounded inlet, radial straightener and progressive-nozzle study as five real parts; the prior 81-instance LED assembly is labelled superseded. See the [inspection record](cad/onshape-inspection.md) and [implementation plan](docs/implementation-plan.md).

Local CAD contains a straight airflow head with an integrated outlet in two structural halves, a rounded bell-mouth, a trial radial straightener, guards, magnetic grille and keepers, a single-servo yoke mechanism, removable linkage cover, horizontal thumbwheel, a clear-window IPS screen, downward ambient lighting and a removable electronics tray. The screen has an actual manufacturer model, removable cradle and insert-mounted bezel; 174 sampled display service-path checks and twelve nominal display fastener checks pass. The current full model passes 5,536 pair checks and 183 cover-removal samples with zero unintended intersections. These are real parametric B-reps with STEP/STL exports, but the routed carrier PCB, interlock mount and complete harness/tubing integration remain unfinished. Files called `REF-` are explicitly approximate envelopes; vendor files have a separate [model register](cad/model-register.md).

The modular Pico firmware compiles, and 7,112 control/encoder assertions, 163 display-view assertions and 384,000 production-GFX pixel comparisons pass with exit 0. It implements normal maximum followed by one full encoder turn to enter boost, then a separate progressive boost adjustment range. Returning boost to zero exits boost; re-entering requires a fresh full turn. The screen's reserved top section replaces the speed/status bars; its lower section shows relative encoder position and degrees remaining before entry. Read [screen and controls](docs/screen-and-controls.md) for the interaction and production-rendered preview.

Feedback/pressure protection, startup, night mode, service calibration and validated settings storage remain implemented. No physical electronics have been tested. Read [firmware operation](docs/firmware-operation.md), [calibration](docs/calibration.md), [BOM](hardware/BOM.md), [wiring](hardware/wiring.md), [power budget](hardware/power-budget.md) and [electrical review](hardware/circuit-review.md). The specified custom carrier circuit still needs schematic capture and PCB layout. [Build provenance](firmware/dist/build-validation.json) records source and UF2 hashes.

Physical airflow, acoustics, servo calibration, protection thresholds, print tolerances, magnet retention and thermal performance must be tested before qualification. [Deliverable status](docs/deliverable-status.md) separates completed work from unfinished engineering and physical tests.
