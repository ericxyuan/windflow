# Purchased CAD register

Manufacturer files are in `vendor/`; download URLs and SHA-256 hashes are in `../hardware/sources.json`. `vendor-checks.json` records eight loaded, valid manufacturer B-reps, including the screen. **Rev D is the current 150 mm CAD candidate**, using these purchased references, a new printed rotor and a compact hollow enclosure. See `../docs/rev-d-150mm-cad.md` and `rev_d/stage-validation.json` for the current geometry and limitations; `onshape-rev-d-checkpoint.json` records online integration when verified. The editable native rotor is a separate profile study from the independently checked P2 fit article.

The new `WindflowRevDHead.fs` native airpath has 17 parameters and passes both open and maximum-closure evaluations with a seven-body assertion. Its source is saved and readback-verified in the current Onshape document's Feature Studio `fcaf5b7ea81e608e05d9abb6`; main-workspace instantiation is still pending renewed browser sign-in. It is distinct from the detailed local split-shell fit article.

The 76-instance Rev B assembly dated 2026-10-03 and 71-instance Rev C import are historical checkpoints, recorded in `onshape-inspection.md` and `onshape-rev-c-checkpoint.json`. Their reports cannot qualify new Rev D geometry, current wiring, physical fit or operation. Earlier faulty Rev B housings were repaired and checked for their own source snapshot. Native mates, all fasteners, PCB population, connected looms, pressure plumbing and the revised grille interlock remain unfinished integration.

## Attempts without a verified exact 3D model

- FEETECH FS90-FB / Pololu #3436: Pololu resources provide the FS90-C family drawing, not an exact FB STEP. The case body is 23.2 x 12.5 x 22; lugs, output spline/horn and fourth wire need a measured sample. A generic SG90 model is not an acceptable exact substitution. [Source](https://www.pololu.com/product/3436/resources).
- Bourns PEC11H-4215F-S0024: exact part listed by SnapMagic and manufacturer drawing obtained. Download access/model verification is pending; do not relabel another EC11 body as this part. Shaft 6 mm with flat at 4.5 mm, length 15 mm from mounting face, M7 x 0.75 bushing, 0.5 +/- 0.3 mm press travel. [Drawing](https://www.bourns.com/docs/product-datasheets/pec11h.pdf), [exact library listing](https://www.snapeda.com/parts/PEC11H-4215F-S0024/Bourns/view-part/).
- Large power and USB-C hardware: actual HUSB238 and regulator CAD downloaded as above. The custom carrier, load-switch circuits and wiring are not yet a fabricated PCB or an integrated CAD model.

## Onshape integration and remaining checks

The original design is preserved in its named baseline. Native parametric bell-mouth, straightener and progressive-nozzle study regenerate as five parts. STEP imports use millimetres and Z-up. The local E3 assembly checkpoint passed 5,536 pair checks for 76 components and 183 outward cover-removal samples across three boost positions, with zero unintended intersections (2026-10-03, source hashes recorded). The current [Onshape checkpoint](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/5ca1b5b26cd9a4a93dde4737/w/6d3d38529391b53ad011b362/e/9ee256003ae02b710e21a9ec) shows 76 instances; its native interference tool reports six overlaps within the supplied HUSB238 model and the intentional encoder/wheel engagement, with no further findings for that revision. The imported combined Part Studio contains 203 bodies because purchased modules contain multiple sub-bodies. The separately imported [encoder P1 datum](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/5ca1b5b26cd9a4a93dde4737/w/6d3d38529391b53ad011b362/e/21041dd778175eaad866a4ab) contains one routed-board part. Assembly motion/mates, complete fasteners, wiring/tubing and tool sweeps remain to complete in the new local revision. This does not establish physical assembly fit.
